# Shopping

> **Source folder:** `src/Shopping/`
> **Status:** Implemented
> **Most recent material milestone:** M30.1 — Shopping ↔ Promotions coupon integration
> **Dossier depth:** S2 — full

## Purpose

Shopping owns the customer's pre-purchase cart lifecycle. A customer adds items to a cart, changes quantities, removes items, applies and removes coupons, and ultimately initiates checkout — at which point the Cart stream reaches a terminal state and the cart contents are handed to Orders as an integration message. Prices are captured at the moment an item is added to the cart and held there until the cart is cleared or checked out, so the price the customer sees on the product page is the price they pay (per ADR 0017).

## Aggregates

### `Cart`

- **Stream ID:** UUID v7, generated by `Guid.CreateVersion7()` inside `InitializeCartHandler.Handle` and passed to `MartenOps.StartStream<Cart>(cartId, …)` (`src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/InitializeCart.cs#L25-L33`). One stream per cart; the cart identifier is the stream identifier.
- **Key state** (fields that drive invariants):
  - `Status` — `CartStatus.Active | Abandoned | Cleared | CheckedOut` (`src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/CartStatus.cs`). The derived property `IsTerminal` returns `true` whenever `Status != Active` (`src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/Cart.cs#L15`); every modifying handler's `Before` checks this and returns a 400 if the cart is terminal.
  - `Items` — `Dictionary<string, CartLineItem>` keyed by SKU (`src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/Cart.cs#L10`). Empty-cart and item-presence checks gate `InitiateCheckout`, `ApplyCouponToCart`, `ChangeItemQuantity`, and `RemoveItemFromCart`.
  - `CartLineItem.UnitPrice` — frozen at the time `ItemAdded` is appended; the unit price is captured from the Pricing BC response inside `AddItemToCart*Handler.Handle` and never re-fetched on subsequent applies (`src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/CartLineItem.cs`, `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/AddItemToCart.cs#L52-L72`). This is the price-freeze invariant from ADR 0017.
  - `AppliedCouponCode` and `AppliedDiscount` — at most one coupon may be applied at a time; `RemoveCouponFromCart.Before` requires `AppliedCouponCode` to be non-null (`src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/RemoveCouponFromCart.cs#L37-L42`).
  - `CustomerId` is nullable; `SessionId` is nullable; `InitializeCartValidator` requires at least one to be supplied (`src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/InitializeCart.cs#L15-L18`).
- **Lifecycle stages:**
  - **Active** — entered on `CartInitialized` (the `Cart.Create` factory in `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/Cart.cs#L17-L23`). All item, quantity, and coupon mutations are accepted while in this state.
  - **Cleared** — entered on `CartCleared`. The apply method also empties `Items` (`src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/Cart.cs#L64-L69`). Terminal — no further commands accepted.
  - **Abandoned** — entered on `CartAbandoned` (`src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/Cart.cs#L71-L72`). No command in the BC currently appends this event; the Apply method exists, the event record is declared, and the Shopping.Api README documents an unimplemented background-job emitter (`src/Shopping/Shopping.Api/README.md#L41`, `#L184`). Terminal.
  - **CheckedOut** — entered on `CheckoutInitiated` (`src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/Cart.cs#L74-L75`). Terminal for the Cart stream; the workflow continues in Orders via the integration message of the same name.
- **File:** `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/Cart.cs`

## Commands

All eight commands target the `Cart` aggregate. None are saga-internal — every command is sent by an external HTTP caller (storefront BFF, Backoffice, or Vendor Portal). The handlers all use Wolverine's compound-handler shape (`Before` for guard checks, optional `ValidateAsync` for HTTP-time external-service validation, `Handle` for the event/outgoing-message return).

- `InitializeCart` — creates a new Cart stream for either a logged-in customer (`CustomerId`) or an anonymous session (`SessionId`); appends `CartInitialized` and returns the new cart id. Handler: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/InitializeCart.cs#L22-L41`.
- `AddItemToCart` — fetches the authoritative `BasePrice` from Pricing for the SKU, then appends `ItemAdded` with that price; the SKU's quantity is summed if the SKU is already in the cart (`src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/Cart.cs#L29-L42`). HTTP path runs `ValidateAsync` first and rejects unknown SKUs and any non-`Published` Pricing status. Handler: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/AddItemToCart.cs#L26-L152`.
- `RemoveItemFromCart` — appends `ItemRemoved` for the named SKU; rejects with 404 if the SKU is not in the cart. Handler: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/RemoveItemFromCart.cs`.
- `ChangeItemQuantity` — captures the prior quantity from the loaded aggregate and appends `ItemQuantityChanged(Sku, OldQuantity, NewQuantity, ChangedAt)`; validator requires `NewQuantity > 0`. Handler: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/ChangeItemQuantity.cs`.
- `ClearCart` — appends `CartCleared(ClearedAt, Reason?)` and transitions the cart to terminal `Cleared`. The HTTP `DELETE /api/carts/{cartId}` endpoint is in a separate static class to keep the command-handler `Before` from being applied to the HTTP path (`src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/ClearCart.cs#L50-L89`).
- `ApplyCouponToCart` — calls Promotions to validate the coupon and to compute `TotalDiscount` against the cart line items, then appends `CouponApplied(CartId, CouponCode, DiscountAmount, AppliedAt)`. HTTP path's `ValidateAsync` rejects coupons that Promotions reports as invalid. Handler: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/ApplyCouponToCart.cs#L24-L167`.
- `RemoveCouponFromCart` — appends `CouponRemoved(CartId, CouponCode, RemovedAt)` carrying the coupon code from the aggregate's `AppliedCouponCode`; `Before` rejects when no coupon is currently applied. Handler: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/RemoveCouponFromCart.cs`.
- `InitiateCheckout` — generates a new `CheckoutId` (UUID v7), appends `CheckoutInitiated` (terminal Cart event), and publishes the `Messages.Contracts.Shopping.CheckoutInitiated` integration message containing the cart's line items to Orders. `Before` rejects empty or terminal carts. Handler: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/InitiateCheckout.cs`.

## Domain events

All nine events live alongside the Cart aggregate in `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/`.

- `CartInitialized(CustomerId, SessionId, InitializedAt)` — a customer or anonymous session opened a cart. File: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/CartInitialized.cs`.
- `ItemAdded(Sku, Quantity, UnitPrice, AddedAt)` — a SKU was added to the cart at the named unit price (frozen). File: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/ItemAdded.cs`.
- `ItemRemoved(Sku, RemovedAt)` — a SKU was removed from the cart. File: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/ItemRemoved.cs`.
- `ItemQuantityChanged(Sku, OldQuantity, NewQuantity, ChangedAt)` — the line-item quantity for a SKU was changed. File: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/ItemQuantityChanged.cs`.
- `CartCleared(ClearedAt, Reason?)` — the cart was emptied and moved to a terminal state. File: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/CartCleared.cs`.
- `CartAbandoned(AbandonedAt)` — the cart was marked abandoned. The event record and `Cart.Apply(CartAbandoned)` exist; no production code path appends this event today (see "Lifecycle stages" above). File: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/CartAbandoned.cs`.
- `CouponApplied(CartId, CouponCode, DiscountAmount, AppliedAt)` — a coupon was applied to the cart with the named monetary discount. File: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/CouponApplied.cs`.
- `CouponRemoved(CartId, CouponCode, RemovedAt)` — the previously applied coupon was removed. File: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/CouponRemoved.cs`.
- `CheckoutInitiated(CartId, CheckoutId, CustomerId, Items, InitiatedAt)` — terminal Cart event; the cart's contents are being handed to Orders. File: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/CheckoutInitiated.cs`.

Count reconciliation: 9 domain events, 8 commands — matches the S1 stub counts in the M48.0 Session 1 retrospective.

## Projections

- `Cart` snapshot — **inline** (`SnapshotLifecycle.Inline`); keyed by the Cart stream id; source events: all nine listed above. Registered via `opts.Projections.Snapshot<Cart>(SnapshotLifecycle.Inline)` in `src/Shopping/Shopping.Api/Program.cs#L41`. Serves the `GET /api/carts/{cartId}` query (the only read endpoint Shopping exposes) by way of `session.Events.AggregateStreamAsync<Cart>(cartId, …)` in `src/Shopping/Shopping.Api/Cart/GetCartEndpoint.cs#L24-L26`. The HTTP response is shaped by `CartResponse.From(Cart)` (`src/Shopping/Shopping.Api/Cart/GetCartEndpoint.cs#L49-L66`), which derives `TotalAmount`, `AppliedDiscount`, and `DiscountedTotal` on read.

No async projections; no document-store read models. The `CartResponse` shape is computed inline at query time, not maintained as a Marten document.

## Integration events

All published from Shopping (no inbound consumers). Routing is configured in `src/Shopping/Shopping.Api/Program.cs#L86-L100`.

**Direction: Shopping → Customer Experience BFF (queue `storefront-notifications`)**

- `Messages.Contracts.Shopping.ItemAdded` — payload: `CartId: Guid, CustomerId: Guid, Sku: string, Quantity: int, UnitPrice: decimal, AddedAt: DateTimeOffset`. Publisher: Shopping.Api → subscriber: Customer Experience BFF (drives storefront SSE/SignalR cart push). File: `src/Shared/Messages.Contracts/Shopping/ItemAdded.cs`.
- `Messages.Contracts.Shopping.ItemRemoved` — payload: `CartId: Guid, CustomerId: Guid, Sku: string, RemovedAt: DateTimeOffset`. Publisher → subscriber: Shopping → Customer Experience. File: `src/Shared/Messages.Contracts/Shopping/ItemRemoved.cs`.
- `Messages.Contracts.Shopping.ItemQuantityChanged` — payload: `CartId: Guid, CustomerId: Guid, Sku: string, OldQuantity: int, NewQuantity: int, ChangedAt: DateTimeOffset`. Publisher → subscriber: Shopping → Customer Experience. File: `src/Shared/Messages.Contracts/Shopping/ItemQuantityChanged.cs`.
- `Messages.Contracts.Shopping.CouponApplied` — payload: `CartId: Guid, CustomerId: Guid, CouponCode: string, DiscountAmount: decimal, AppliedAt: DateTimeOffset`. Publisher → subscriber: Shopping → Customer Experience. File: `src/Shared/Messages.Contracts/Shopping/CouponApplied.cs`.
- `Messages.Contracts.Shopping.CouponRemoved` — payload: `CartId: Guid, CustomerId: Guid, CouponCode: string, RemovedAt: DateTimeOffset`. Publisher → subscriber: Shopping → Customer Experience. File: `src/Shared/Messages.Contracts/Shopping/CouponRemoved.cs`.

**Direction: Shopping → Orders (queue `orders-checkout-initiated`)**

- `Messages.Contracts.Shopping.CheckoutInitiated` — payload: `CheckoutId: Guid, CartId: Guid, CustomerId: Guid?, Items: IReadOnlyList<CheckoutLineItem>, InitiatedAt: DateTimeOffset`, where `CheckoutLineItem` is `(Sku: string, Quantity: int, UnitPrice: decimal)`. Publisher: Shopping → subscriber: Orders (consumed to start the Orders `Checkout` aggregate per ADR 0001). Files: `src/Shared/Messages.Contracts/Shopping/CheckoutInitiated.cs`, `src/Shared/Messages.Contracts/Shopping/CheckoutLineItem.cs`.

**Direction: Shopping ← (declared, not subscribed in this BC)**

- `Messages.Contracts.Shopping.CartCheckoutCompleted` — payload: `OrderId: Guid, CheckoutId: Guid, CustomerId: Guid?, Items: IReadOnlyList<CheckoutLineItem>, ShippingAddress: AddressSnapshot, ShippingMethod: string, ShippingCost: decimal, PaymentMethodToken: string, CompletedAt: DateTimeOffset`. The contract is declared in the Shopping namespace under `src/Shared/Messages.Contracts/Shopping/CartCheckoutCompleted.cs`; `Shopping.Api/Program.cs` does not register a publisher or subscriber for it. The completion notification is emitted from the post-checkout flow that Orders owns.

Shopping does not subscribe to any external integration events; data from Pricing and Promotions enters via synchronous HTTP calls (see "HTTP / API surface").

## Sagas / orchestration

Not applicable — this BC is not a saga orchestrator and does not participate in another BC's saga as a long-running participant. Cart is a single-aggregate event-sourced workflow that terminates with `CheckoutInitiated` and hands off to the Orders saga via one outbound integration message.

## HTTP / API surface

All routes are declared with `[WolverinePost]`, `[WolverinePut]`, `[WolverineDelete]`, or `[WolverineGet]` attributes on handler/endpoint methods. The `Shopping.Api` host project is `src/Shopping/Shopping.Api/`.

**Cart commands:**

- `POST /api/carts` — initialize a new cart. Handler: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/InitializeCart.cs#L24`. Auth: anonymous (no `[Authorize]`; the storefront calls this for guest sessions).
- `POST /api/carts/{cartId}/items` — add an item; runs `ValidateAsync` against Pricing first. Handler: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/AddItemToCart.cs#L125`. Auth: anonymous.
- `PUT /api/carts/{cartId}/items/{sku}/quantity` — change line-item quantity. Handler: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/ChangeItemQuantity.cs#L51`. Auth: anonymous.
- `DELETE /api/carts/{cartId}/items/{sku}` — remove a line item. Handler: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/RemoveItemFromCart.cs#L35`. Auth: anonymous.
- `DELETE /api/carts/{cartId}` — clear the cart. Handler: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/ClearCart.cs#L79`. Auth: anonymous.
- `POST /api/carts/{cartId}/apply-coupon` — apply a coupon; runs `ValidateAsync` against Promotions first. Handler: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/ApplyCouponToCart.cs#L134`. Auth: anonymous.
- `DELETE /api/carts/{cartId}/apply-coupon` — remove the applied coupon. Handler: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/RemoveCouponFromCart.cs#L94`. Auth: anonymous.
- `POST /api/carts/{cartId}/checkout` — terminal command: appends `CheckoutInitiated` and publishes `Messages.Contracts.Shopping.CheckoutInitiated`. Handler: `src/Shopping/Shopping/Cart/InitiateCheckout.cs#L47`. Auth: anonymous.

**Cart query:**

- `GET /api/carts/{cartId}` — returns the current `CartResponse` (rebuilt from the snapshot via `AggregateStreamAsync<Cart>`). Handler: `src/Shopping/Shopping.Api/Cart/GetCartEndpoint.cs#L18`. Auth: anonymous.

**Endpoints consumed by other BCs (per CONTEXTS.md and verified against this surface):**

- Customer Experience BFF queries `GET /api/carts/{cartId}` to compose the storefront cart view.
- Orders does not query Shopping endpoints; the cart contents reach Orders via the `CheckoutInitiated` integration message rather than a pull.

**Outbound HTTP clients consumed by Shopping** (declared as typed interfaces in `src/Shopping/Shopping/Clients/`, implemented in `src/Shopping/Shopping.Api/Clients/`):

- `IPricingClient.GetPriceAsync(sku)` → Pricing.Api `GET` for current price; called from `AddItemToCart` handlers (`src/Shopping/Shopping/Clients/IPricingClient.cs`).
- `IPromotionsClient.ValidateCouponAsync` and `CalculateDiscountAsync` → Promotions.Api; called from `ApplyCouponToCart` handlers (`src/Shopping/Shopping/Clients/IPromotionsClient.cs`).
- A `CustomerIdentity` named `HttpClient` is registered at `src/Shopping/Shopping.Api/Program.cs#L107-L113` but no typed client uses it from this BC at present.

- **Scheme:** Two `JwtBearer` schemes are registered side by side — `"Backoffice"` (authority `https://localhost:5249`) and `"Vendor"` (authority `https://localhost:5240`) — at `src/Shopping/Shopping.Api/Program.cs#L140-L166`. Authorization middleware is wired (`UseAuthentication`/`UseAuthorization`, `src/Shopping/Shopping.Api/Program.cs#L190-L191`).
- **Policies:** None registered; `AddAuthorization()` is called without per-policy configuration (`src/Shopping/Shopping.Api/Program.cs#L168`).
- **Roles:** Both JWT schemes set `RoleClaimType = "role"`; no role-restricted endpoints are declared today.
- **Endpoint posture:** None of the cart endpoints carry `[Authorize]`. Storefront callers (anonymous sessions and authenticated customers) reach the cart endpoints without a bearer token; the Backoffice/Vendor schemes are present for future role-protected operations on this surface.
- **Source files:** `src/Shopping/Shopping.Api/Program.cs` (auth registration); per-endpoint files cited above (no per-endpoint auth attributes).

## ADRs

- **ADR 0001 — Checkout Migration to Orders BC.** Established that the `Checkout` aggregate lives in Orders, and that Shopping hands off via the `CheckoutInitiated` integration message. This dossier's terminal Cart event and the `orders-checkout-initiated` queue both originate in this decision. File: `docs/decisions/0001-checkout-migration-to-orders.md`.
- **ADR 0017 — Price Freeze at Add-to-Cart (Not Checkout).** Established that the unit price written into `CartLineItem` is the authoritative Pricing BC price at add-to-cart time, and is held immutable for the life of the cart. This is the invariant enforced by `AddItemToCart*Handler.Handle` and persisted through `Cart.Apply(ItemAdded)`. The ADR also describes a `PriceFrozenAt` field and a TTL-driven `PriceRefreshed` flow; neither is present in `CartLineItem.cs` or in any Shopping handler today. File: `docs/decisions/0017-price-freeze-at-add-to-cart.md`.
